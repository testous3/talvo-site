Features

Everything you need to cultivate a calmer mind

Talvo is free, with optional premium visual themes.

🌱

Personalized affirmations feed

11 categories — confidence, calm, motivation, self-love, gratitude, inner strength, relationships, career, joy, rebuilding, wellness tips. Like them, find them again in your library.

📈

Mood tracking

Answer in one tap how you're feeling each day and see your progress over 30 days, with a visual trend and a weekly summary.

🎧

Built-in ambient sounds

Each category has its own ambient sound, available offline — no connection needed to enjoy it.

🎨

Visual themes

Personalize your feed's background with photo packs (nature, soft, night, minimal) or stick with Talvo's signature colors.

🔥

Streaks and badges

Come back every day to grow your streak, and unlock badges as you progress.

✨

AI-personalized affirmations

Write down a thought that's weighing on you, and let Talvo reframe it into a positive affirmation, tailored to you.

🌍

Available in several languages

French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Arabic.
